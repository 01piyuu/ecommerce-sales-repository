## Core Business Questions Answered

* *What are the top-line performance metrics?*  
  Total Revenue: *$115.56M* | Net Profit: *$19.77M* | Overall Margin: *17.11%* | Total Volume: *8,812 units* across *2,500 orders*.
* *How are sales and profits trending over time?*  
  24-month dual-axis monthly area and line trends reveal steady baseline revenue with pronounced seasonal mid-year and year-end demand spikes.
* *Which regions and channels drive top-line value?*  
  Identifies key regional contributors and breaks down channel distribution across Retail, Wholesale, Direct, and Online Marketplace channels.
* *Where are the margin leaks?*  
  High-ticket categories (Electronics and Furniture) drive the largest gross revenue share but carry compressed percentage margins, whereas smaller categories yield higher margin efficiency.
* *Which customer cohorts drive retention value?*  
  Returning customers generate substantially higher aggregate transaction volume and margin quality than first-time acquisitions across Consumer, Corporate, and Small Business segments.
* *Who are the top performers?*  
  Identifies sales rep distribution, led by Meera, Ananya, and Diya, with balanced volume across the wider sales team.
